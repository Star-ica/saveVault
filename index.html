Why SafeVault

Treasury security that scales with your team

M-of-N Multisig

Define any approval threshold. 2-of-3, 4-of-7, 10-of-15 — your governance rules, enforced by code not trust.

Proposal Lifecycle

Every action — from token transfers to signer changes — goes through a transparent on-chain proposal with full audit trail.

No Backdoors

Signer management is itself governed by proposals. No privileged admin can bypass the approval threshold — ever.

Contract Calls

Execute arbitrary smart contract interactions with calldata, not just simple transfers. Full DeFi composability.

Token Support

Hold and transfer native BOT alongside any ERC-20 token. SafeERC20 patterns protect every token operation.

Deadline Controls

Set expiry timestamps on proposals. Time-sensitive governance actions automatically expire, preventing stale approvals.
